Our Leader

Ivana Jandrić

European & World Champion, CEO of Klub Borac, and driving force behind sports inclusion in Serbia

From Champion to Change-Maker

CEO & Head Coach | FIAS Athletes' Commission Member

Ivana Jandrić is one of Serbia's most decorated sambists and judokas. Born and raised in Novi Sad, she discovered sambo at age 17 and quickly rose to become a dominant force on the international stage.

After retiring from competitive sport following the 2021 World Championships in Tashkent, Ivana transitioned into coaching and club leadership. She now serves as President of the Sambo Federation of Vojvodina region and as a coach for the Serbian national team, while leading Klub Borac as its CEO.

Her vision extends beyond medals — through the Borac Active Program, she brings martial arts to underserved communities, using sport as a vehicle for inclusion, gender equity, and social change.

Social Impact

Borac Active Program

Bringing martial arts to communities. Building champions beyond the mat.

Sport as a Vehicle for Change

The Borac Active Program (BAP) is our flagship social initiative that takes martial arts beyond the dojo and into the heart of communities across Serbia. We believe that every child, regardless of background, deserves access to the transformative power of sport.

Through BAP, we work with vulnerable populations, minority communities, and children from disadvantaged backgrounds — using judo and sambo as tools for building discipline, confidence, and social inclusion.

Community Outreach

Free training programs in underserved neighborhoods, bringing professional coaching to children who otherwise couldn't afford it.

Gender Equity

Actively promoting women's participation in combat sports, with dedicated girls' programs and female role models leading by example.

Youth Development

Life skills through martial arts — teaching discipline, respect, and perseverance to shape the next generation of leaders.

Minority Inclusion

Special programs for Roma communities, refugees, and other minority groups, creating bridges through shared athletic pursuit.

In 2026, Klub Borac was honored with the prestigious Erasmus #BeActive EU Sport Award from the European Commission — recognizing our outstanding contribution to promoting physical activity, social inclusion, and shared European values through sport.
